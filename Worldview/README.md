# Worldview

This is the ledger for what the project holds about ToolBook II 8.1.
The binder tagged `v1.0.0r2` is the stable print of this ledger.
Edits after that tag are experimental until the next release. The binder is rebuilt only then.
`v1.0.0` and `v1.0.0r1` are the previous prints.

The older chapters stay. They are the narrative. When a chapter and the ledger disagree, the ledger wins.

The printable binder is the published release. It is rebuilt when that release is cut, and not when this ledger changes.

## Grades

| Grade | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Known | Seen in a binary, a book, or a live run, and the same observation can be repeated. |
| Thought | The model used to choose the next experiment. It fits the known facts. It is not one of them. |
| Conjecture | A guess. It has not been tested, or the test did not settle it. |
| Not true | It was believed, and a later observation killed it. It does not come back without new evidence. |

A further crash is not a higher grade. A reader that shows text is not the engine opening the book. A stub that returns a convenient value has not read that value out of the binary.

## Add

1. Take the next free id. Ids are `WV-` and a number. They are never reused and never renumbered.
2. Put the entry under its grade.
3. Write the statement in one sentence, then the evidence, then what would falsify it.
4. Date it.

## Remove

Do not delete an entry. Change its grade to Not true. Keep the id, the old statement, why it was believed, and the observation that killed it. Move the block under Not true.

## Upgrade or correct

Keep the id. Change the grade and say what changed. If the wording was wrong, leave the old wording in the history line. Do not quietly replace a claim with a different one under the same words.

## What does not belong here

Personal names, mail addresses, and the Knowledge Base host. The correspondent is JohnDoe1 in every entry that needs one. Machine paths and home directories do not belong here either. Cite a repository and a commit.
