# Draft 1.1.0

Not a release. Do not tag this. `v1.0.0` stays the published release until that tag is asked for.

## What this draft adds

- `Worldview/README.md` — how a claim is added, corrected, or retired without deleting it. The ledger is the experimental build. The binder is the stable one.
- `Worldview/ledger.md` — known, thought, conjecture, and not true, with stable ids.
- `Worldview/environments.md` — how Win16, Windows 98, and Windows XP look to ToolBook 8.1.
- `Worldview/audience.md` — who published sources say used it, and who would still have a reason to care.

## What this draft does not do

- It does not rebuild `_production/TBWorldview_printable.pdf`. The binder is rebuilt only when a release is cut. Until then it stays the `v1.0.0` print.
- It does not retag the module lists or the older PDFs.
- It does not contain personal names. JohnDoe1 remains the only mask.

## Why a ledger

The narrative chapters accumulated claims that later turned out to be false, and the false ones were easy to repeat. The ledger is the list that gets edited. A retired claim keeps its id and moves to Not true. A new observation takes the next id. Nothing is renumbered.
