# Knowledge Base findings (2026-10-19)

Extract of the ToolBook Knowledge Base that Denny Dedmore preserved and hosts at https://dennydedmore.com/toolbook/knowledgebase/kb/ after the 31 December 2021 EOL. He created the original Knowledge Base while at SumTotal. The material is public.

## Activation

No new activations are possible. Existing activations on already-activated machines continue. There is no bypass. The 30-day trial still runs on a fresh install but cannot be converted to a full license.

## 11.5 Patch 4 (4 August 2015)

Supersedes prior patches. The base installer does not include it. Fixes cover several DHTML publishing errors, IE11 compatibility, Universal Media Player Time Markers, the bookmarking checkbox, Chapter Title length (64 → 255), a false SCORE Actions Editor warning, the About dialog patch level, and Microsoft Edge handling.

## Corruption recovery

The Repair Kit copies objects, pages, backgrounds, and resources into a new book and removes many forms of corruption. Alternative methods are page-by-page copy/paste into a blank book and selective Insert → Pages.

Full notes: `SME/kb-notes.md`.

These are historical support facts. They do not alter the 8.1 binary ledger entries.
